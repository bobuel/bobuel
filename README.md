# Alex Aidun

I make complex systems easier to use. I design tools and workflows, help people put AI to work, and build games to explore ideas.

I start with a real task, build something others can reuse, and change the tools when they get in the way. I care about what we choose to build and whether it helps people do the work.

**Design is the premium.**

Selected work: [AI tools and adoption at Automattic](https://aydoon.com/case-studies/enterprise-ai-adoption-automattic) · [AI product leadership at Dremio](https://aydoon.com/case-studies/ai-product-leadership-dremio).

## Plugins / Skills

- **[Bloom Quiz Builder](https://github.com/bobuel/bloom-taxonomy-quiz-builder-skill)** — turn source material into questions across Bloom’s six levels, with answer evidence and teacher review. [Case study](https://aydoon.com/case-studies/bloom-assessment-workflow).
- **[Certification Workshop](https://github.com/bobuel/certification-workshop)** — build and maintain certification programs, from source material and job tasks through assessments, review, and pilots. [Case study](https://aydoon.com/case-studies/certification-workshop).
- **[Eventer](https://github.com/bobuel/eventer-plugin)** — plan group trips, retreats, and weekends, keeping guests, rooms, meals, and rides aligned as plans change. [Case study](https://aydoon.com/case-studies/eventer-planning-workflow).
